Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DD Month YYYY] — Smart Flashlight (com.amey.smartflashlight)

This policy explains what Smart Flashlight does and doesn't collect. The app is built to work without accounts, sign-ins, or personal data collection.

Permissions the app uses

  • Camera / Flash: used only to switch the device's LED torch on and off. The app does not capture, store, or transmit photos or video.

Advertising

Smart Flashlight shows ads served by Google AdMob to keep the app free. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), general device information, and approximate location to serve and measure ads. This data is handled under Google's own privacy policy, not by us directly.

You can review and reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, from your device's system settings.

Learn more about how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Data we do not collect

  • No account or sign-in is required to use the app.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, or messages.
  • We do not sell any data.

Children's privacy

Smart Flashlight is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
